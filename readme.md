**Focus Garden 🌱**

Focus Garden is a simple productivity app that makes staying focused a little more fun. It uses a Pomodoro timer to help you work in focused sessions, take breaks, and build better study habits. The fun part is that your plant grows as you complete focus sessions, and every finished session adds a new plant to your personal garden.

The app includes customizable timers, session tracking, sound notifications, and saved progress, all wrapped in a calm, nature-inspired design. It’s built using HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript, with no backend or internet connection needed. The idea is simple: focus a little every day and watch your garden grow along with you.
